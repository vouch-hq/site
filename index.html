Questions people ask

When does it launch?

Sydney first, on the App Store later this year. Friends of the team are testing it now. Leave your email above and you'll hear the day it's live.

Does it cost anything?

Vouch is free to use. Your feed is your friends' places and nothing else.

Who can see my places?

You're private by default. You add friends on purpose, and what you write stays with the people you choose to share it with.

Can I bring in the places I've already saved?

Yes. Import your Google Maps saved places and they land on your map on day one.

What about other cities?

Sydney until it's dense enough that a friend's recommendation is always nearby, then Melbourne on the same playbook.
